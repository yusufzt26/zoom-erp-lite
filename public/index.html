Sales orders

Make a sales order from a won quotation (open it on the Sales page), or start one here. Challans and invoices are made from the order, so you can see what is still to dispatch and bill.

Delivery challans

Open a sales order and press "Make challan" to send part or all of it. Pending quantity is tracked per line.

Tax invoices

Make the invoice from a challan or a sales order. GST is CGST + SGST for Maharashtra customers (GSTIN starting 27) and IGST for others. Record payments received to track what is still to collect.
